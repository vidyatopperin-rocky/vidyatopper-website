1. The French Revolution & the Idea of the Nation

The French Revolution of 1789 marked the first clear political expression of modern nationalism. To foster a collective identity (le citoyen and la patrie) among French citizens, revolutionaries introduced:

  • A new tricolor French flag replacing the royal standard.
  • Estate General renamed the National Assembly, elected by active citizens.
  • Uniform system of weights and measures; internal customs duties abolished.
  • French language standardized across Paris; regional dialects discouraged.
  • A centralized administrative system framing uniform laws for all citizens.

2. Napoleonic Civil Code of 1804 & Its Spread

Napoleon Bonaparte revolutionized administration through the Civil Code of 1804 (Napoleonic Code):

  • Abolished all privileges based on birth.
  • Established legal equality before the law for all male citizens.
  • Secured and codified the universal right to private property.
  • Abolished feudal dues and serfdom across Dutch Republic, Switzerland, and Italy.
  • Guild restrictions in towns were removed; transport and communication networks modernized.

Initial Enthusiasm Turned Hostile: Conquered populations turned hostile when increased taxation, military conscription, and censorship outweighed administrative benefits.

3. Unification of Germany & Italy

Unification of Germany (1866–1871):

  • Led by Prussian Chief Minister Otto von Bismarck using his policy of Blood and Iron.
  • Three wars over seven years with Austria, Denmark, and France ended in Prussian victory.
  • In January 1871, Prussian King Kaiser William I was proclaimed German Emperor in the Hall of Mirrors at Versailles.

Unification of Italy:

  • Giuseppe Mazzini: Formed secret societies (Young Italy, Young Europe) to propagate republican unification.
  • Count Camillo de Cavour: Diplomatic architect; engineered alliance with France to defeat Austrian forces in 1859.
  • Giuseppe Garibaldi: Led the volunteer "Red Shirts" into the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies, winning peasant support.
  • In 1861, Victor Emmanuel II was crowned King of Unified Italy.

💡 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ What was the Zollverein and why was it formed in 1834?

Zollverein was a customs union formed at the initiative of Prussia and joined by most German states in 1834. It abolished internal tariff barriers and reduced the number of separate currencies from over thirty to just two, stimulating commercial mobility and creating economic nationalism.

❓ Why did the Balkan region become the source of intense nationalist tension after 1871?

The Balkans was a region of immense geographical and ethnic diversity (modern Romania, Bulgaria, Serbia, Greece) under Ottoman imperial control. With the disintegration of the Ottoman Empire and spread of romantic nationalism, rival Slavic nationalities competed aggressively for territory, turning the Balkans into a powder keg that eventually precipitated World War I.
