❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are all study materials on Vidya Topper really free?

Yes! Every NCERT solution, formula sheet, sample paper, and quiz is 100% unlocked for all students. There are no fees or hidden subscriptions.

Q: How do I report a mistake or typo in a chapter note?

Select "NCERT & Notes Feedback" in the form above and mention the Subject, Class, and Chapter name. Our subject teachers will verify and update the material promptly.

Q: Can teachers use Vidya Topper in their classrooms?

Absolutely. Educators and coaching teachers are welcome to use our free materials, formula sheets, and chapter quizzes to support their students.
