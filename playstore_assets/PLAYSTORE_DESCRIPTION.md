## 📝 FULL DESCRIPTION (3,723 / 4,000 Characters - Fully Compliant with Accessibility API & Gov Disclaimer)

Vidya Topper is India's all-in-one smart learning app for Class 6 to 12 (CBSE, ICSE & State Boards) and competitive exams. Study NCERT solutions, solve doubts with AI, watch 60s concept shorts, and beat social media distraction with India's first Study Shield & Reels Counter!

🔥 KEY FEATURES:

📸 1. AI Doubt Solver & Camera Snap (Gemini AI)
• Instant OCR: Snap any question to get instant step-by-step solutions.
• AI Smart Teacher: Detailed chapter concepts with Easy, Med & Hard modes.
• AI PDF Reader: Upload textbook PDFs to extract chapter notes & formulas.
• Paper Evaluator: Grade handwritten answers on Board marking schemes.

📚 2. Complete NCERT Solutions (Class 6-12)
• Chapter-wise solutions for Math, Science, Social Science, Hindi & English.
• Covers CBSE, UP Board (UPMSP), Bihar Board (BSEB) & MP Board (MPBSE).
• Previous years' papers (PYQs), sample papers & topper answer sheets.
• Offline Mode: Download notes & books to study without internet.

⚡ 3. 60s Educational Shorts & Video Lectures
• Quick, bite-sized 60-second video concepts for fast revision.
• Tap on any short to watch the complete in-depth video lecture.

🛡️ 4. Reels Counter & Study Shield (Beat Distraction)
• Live Reel Tracker: Monitors daily short video scroll across apps.
• Daily Limit: Set daily limits (e.g. 20 reels/day) to stay focused.
• Floating Counter: Real-time screen awareness while studying.

🏆 5. Hall of Fame, Quizzes & Coins Store
• All-India Leaderboard: Compete on weekly XP rankings to reach #1!
• Earn Coins & Streaks: Daily study streaks, quizzes & Lucky Spin wheel.
• Rewards Store: Redeem coins for study perks and VIP Pro passes.

🎯 WHO IS IT FOR?
• Class 6-12 students (CBSE, ICSE, UP, Bihar, MP Boards).
• Board exam candidates aiming for 95%+ marks.
• Aspirants preparing for JEE, NEET, CUET, NDA, SSC & Railway exams.

🔒 ACCESSIBILITYSERVICE API DISCLOSURE:
Vidya Topper uses Android's AccessibilityService API strictly for its optional "Study Shield & Reels Counter" feature.
• Purpose: To detect scroll events inside short-video apps (Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts) to calculate daily reels watched, show a floating focus pill, and help students avoid doomscrolling during study hours.
• Privacy & Data: We DO NOT read, collect, monitor, store, or share any personal user data, messages, passwords, or keystrokes.
• User Control: This permission is completely optional, requires explicit user activation in Android Settings, and can be turned off at any time.
