🧪 Acids, Bases and Salts: Master Class 10 Chemistry Notes

Everything you need to know about the pH scale, indicators, and commercially important chemical salts.

Academic Session 2025–2026 | Board Exam Ready
Home › Study Materials Hub › Acids, Bases & Salts
🎯 High-Weightage Topic: Commercial salts (Baking Soda, Bleaching Powder, Washing Soda, and Plaster of Paris) account for a guaranteed 4–5 marks in section C or D of the CBSE Science board paper.

1. Chemical Properties of Acids and Bases

Acids are sour in taste, turn blue litmus red, and dissociate in water to produce Hydronium ions (H₃O⁺ or H⁺(aq)). Bases are bitter, soapy to touch, turn red litmus blue, and yield Hydroxide ions (OH⁻(aq)) in solution.

Fundamental Chemical Reactions:

  • Acid + Metal → Salt + Hydrogen Gas: 2HCl(aq) + Zn(s) → ZnCl₂(aq) + H₂(g)↑ (Pop-sound test confirms H₂ gas).
  • Base + Metal → Salt + Hydrogen Gas: 2NaOH(aq) + Zn(s) → Na₂ZnO₂(aq) [Sodium Zincate] + H₂(g)↑.
  • Acid + Metal Carbonate → Salt + Carbon Dioxide + Water: 2HCl + Na₂CO₃ → 2NaCl + H₂O + CO₂↑ (Turns lime water milky due to CaCO₃ precipitate; excess CO₂ clears the solution forming soluble Ca(HCO₃)₂).
  • Neutralization Reaction: Acid + Base → Salt + Water. HCl(aq) + NaOH(aq) → NaCl(aq) + H₂O(l).
  • Metallic Oxide + Acid → Salt + Water: Metallic oxides are basic in nature (e.g., CuO + 2HCl → CuCl₂ [Blue-green] + H₂O). Non-metallic oxides are acidic in nature (e.g., CO₂ + Ca(OH)₂ → CaCO₃ + H₂O).

2. The pH Scale & Importance in Everyday Life

The pH scale measures Hydrogen ion concentration in an aqueous medium. Calculated as pH = -log₁₀[H⁺]. The scale ranges from 0 (extremely acidic) to 14 (extremely alkaline), with 7 being neutral at 25°C.

Crucial Real-World Applications:

  • Human Body pH: Operates within a narrow pH range of 7.0 to 7.8.
  • Acid Rain: When rainwater pH falls below 5.6, it harms aquatic fauna and dissolves historical monuments (stone leprosy, as seen in the Taj Mahal).
  • Digestive System: Stomach produces hydrochloric acid (pH ~1.2) for pepsin activity. Excess acidity causes heartburn, cured using antacids like Milk of Magnesia (Mg(OH)₂) or Sodium Hydrogen Carbonate.
  • Tooth Decay: Begins when mouth pH drops below 5.5. Bacteria degrade food sugars into acids that corrode tooth enamel (calcium hydroxyapatite). Toothpaste is mildly basic to neutralize this acid.
  • Self-defense by Plants & Animals: Honeybee stings inject formic/methanoic acid (cured with mild base like baking soda). Nettle plant leaves inject methanoic acid, relieved by rubbing leaves of the dock plant.

3. Commercially Important Salts: Formulas & Preparation

Sodium Chloride (Common Salt, NaCl) serves as a vital raw material for synthesizing several crucial industrial compounds:

Chemical Name Formula Preparation Reaction Key Industrial Uses
Bleaching Powder CaOCl₂ Dry slaked lime + Chlorine gas:
Ca(OH)₂ + Cl₂ → CaOCl₂ + H₂O		 Textile bleaching, disinfectant for drinking water, oxidizer in chemical plants.
Baking Soda NaHCO₃ Solvay process:
NaCl + H₂O + CO₂ + NH₃ → NH₄Cl + NaHCO₃		 Baking powder (mixed with tartaric acid), antacid medicine, soda-acid fire extinguishers.
Washing Soda Na₂CO₃·10H₂O Recrystallization of sodium carbonate:
Na₂CO₃ + 10H₂O → Na₂CO₃·10H₂O		 Glass, soap and paper industries, removing permanent hardness of water.

CaSO₄·2H₂O --[373K]--> CaSO₄·½H₂O + 1½H₂O		 Fractured bone casts, decorative false ceilings, architectural statues and molds.

💡 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Why should Plaster of Paris be stored in a moisture-proof container?

Plaster of Paris (Calcium Sulphate Hemihydrate) is highly hygroscopic. In the presence of moisture or water, it absorbs water and recrystallizes back into Gypsum, forming a solid hard mass and losing its setting property: CaSO₄·½H₂O + 1½H₂O → CaSO₄·2H₂O.

❓ Why does dry HCl gas not change the color of dry litmus paper?

Acids exhibit acidic properties and turn blue litmus red only in the presence of water, where they ionize to produce free Hydronium (H₃O⁺) ions. In dry gaseous form without moisture, HCl molecules do not dissociate, so no color change occurs.
