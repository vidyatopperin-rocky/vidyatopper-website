📐 Trigonometry & Identities: Formulas, Values & Proof Strategies

Comprehensive guide to right-triangle trigonometry ratios, angle values table, and algebraic identity proofs.

Academic Session 2025–2026 | Board Ready
Home › Study Materials Hub › Trigonometry & Identities
🎯 Three Golden Identities: (1) sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; (2) 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ; (3) 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ. Memorize all rearranged forms for identity proofs.

1. Trigonometric Ratios (T-Ratios) in a Right Triangle

For a right-angled triangle ΔABC with right angle at B and acute angle θ = ∠A:

  • sin θ = Opposite Side (Perpendicular) / Hypotenuse = P / H
  • cos θ = Adjacent Side (Base) / Hypotenuse = B / H
  • tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent = P / B = sin θ / cos θ
  • cosec θ = 1 / sin θ = H / P
  • sec θ = 1 / cos θ = H / B
  • cot θ = 1 / tan θ = B / P = cos θ / sin θ

2. Standard Trigonometric Values Table (0° to 90°)

3. Fundamental Identities & Top Proving Strategies

The 3 Fundamental Identities:

  1. sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 ⟹ sin²θ = 1 - cos²θ ⟹ cos²θ = 1 - sin²θ
  2. 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ ⟹ sec²θ - tan²θ = 1 ⟹ (sec θ - tan θ)(sec θ + tan θ) = 1
  3. 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ ⟹ cosec²θ - cot²θ = 1 ⟹ (cosec θ - cot θ)(cosec θ + cot θ) = 1

4 Rules of Thumb for Identity Proof Questions:

  • Convert everything into terms of sin θ and cos θ when you are stuck.
  • Take the Lowest Common Denominator (LCM) of fractional terms.
  • Rationalize the numerator or denominator when terms like (1 ± sin θ) or (1 ± cos θ) appear inside square roots.
  • Apply algebraic identities: a² - b² = (a - b)(a + b), a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² - ab + b²).

💡 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ Prove that (sin θ - 2sin³θ) / (2cos³θ - cos θ) = tan θ.

LHS = [sin θ(1 - 2sin²θ)] / [cos θ(2cos²θ - 1)]. Since 1 - 2sin²θ = (cos²θ + sin²θ) - 2sin²θ = cos²θ - sin²θ, and 2cos²θ - 1 = 2cos²θ - (cos²θ + sin²θ) = cos²θ - sin²θ. Both bracket terms cancel out completely! LHS = sin θ / cos θ = tan θ = RHS. (Hence Proved)

❓ If tan A = 4/3, find all other trigonometric ratios of angle A.

Given tan A = P/B = 4/3. Let P = 4k, B = 3k. Hypotenuse H = √(P² + B²) = √(16k² + 9k²) = 5k. Therefore: sin A = 4/5, cos A = 3/5, cosec A = 5/4, sec A = 5/3, cot A = 3/4.
