✍️ Class 10 English Formal Letter Writing: Complete Format & Samples

Score full 5/5 marks in Section B Writing Skills with updated CBSE layouts and templates.

💡 CBSE 5-Mark Distribution: Format (Sender address, Date, Receiver address, Subject, Salutation, Complimentary close) = 1 Mark; Content = 2 Marks; Organization of ideas & Accuracy/Grammar = 2 Marks.

1. Standard CBSE Formal Letter Layout

Sender’s Address (2–3 lines, no comma at end)
E.g., 42, Green Park Avenue
New Delhi - 110016

Date (Formal format: 15 October 2025)

The Receiver’s Designation & Office Address
The Editor
The Times of India
Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg, New Delhi

Subject: [Concise, underlined/bold, 6–8 words max]

Sir / Madam,

Body of the Letter:
• Paragraph 1: Introduction (Purpose of writing)
• Paragraph 2: Detailed description of cause, impact, and repercussions
• Paragraph 3: Expected solution, action, or civic appeal

Thanking you,

Yours sincerely / Yours faithfully,
[Signature / Full Name]
[Designation, if applicable]

2. High-Scoring Solved Sample: Letter to the Editor

Prompt: You are Aman / Ananya, residing at 12/B, Shastri Nagar, Kanpur. Write a letter to the Editor of a national daily highlighting the menace of open garbage dumping in your locality and urging civic authorities to act.

12/B, Shastri Nagar
Kanpur - 208005
24 September 2025


The Editor
The Indian Express
Civil Lines, Kanpur


Subject: Menace of open garbage dumps and deteriorating sanitation in Shastri Nagar


Sir,


Through the esteemed columns of your widely circulated newspaper, I wish to draw the urgent attention of the Kanpur Municipal Corporation and health authorities towards the alarming sanitation crisis in Shastri Nagar.

For the past three months, vacant community plots in our locality have transformed into open, unmonitored garbage dumping grounds. Household refuse, plastic debris, and toxic wastes are dumped indiscriminately. The municipal waste collection van visits only once a week instead of daily. Consequently, rotting garbage emits a foul stench, making it impossible for residents to walk outside. Stagnant rainwater in these heaps has turned the area into a rampant breeding ground for mosquitoes, causing a sharp spike in dengue and malaria cases among children.

Despite multiple petitions submitted to the local sanitary inspector, no concrete remediation has been undertaken. It is requested that the municipal authorities immediately clear the existing dumps, place covered communal bins, penalize illegal dumping, and enforce regular door-to-door waste collection.

I sincerely hope this publication awakens the civic authorities from their slumber.


Yours faithfully,
Aman Sharma
Concerned Citizen

3. Pro Tips to Avoid Losing Marks in English Writing Skills

  • Never write "Yours'": Write Yours sincerely. There is no apostrophe in Yours.
  • Subject Line must be precise: Do not write a long paragraph. "Subject: Complaint regarding defective smart TV" is ideal.
  • Adopt a dignified, formal tone: Avoid emotional slang, exclamation marks, or informal abbreviations (don't write gonna, can't, wanna).
  • Maintain strict paragraph structure: Never write the entire letter as one giant continuous block of text; always split into 3 logical paragraphs.

💡 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

❓ When should I use "Yours sincerely" versus "Yours faithfully"?

If you know the recipient by name (Dear Mr. Verma), close with "Yours sincerely". If you address the recipient anonymously by title (Dear Sir / Madam, The Editor, The Principal), close with "Yours faithfully". Both are acceptable in CBSE evaluation provided spelling and punctuation are correct.

❓ What is the ideal word limit for a Class 10 formal letter?

The prescribed word limit is 100 to 120 words. Writing significantly below 80 words leads to content deduction, while exceeding 150 words risks grammatical mistakes and wastes precious exam time.
