✅ Simple Summary: We collect only what is strictly necessary to run the app. We never sell your personal data to anyone. You maintain full ownership of your notes and can delete your account anytime.

1. 📥 What Information We Collect

When you use Vidya Topper, we may process the following types of information:

a) Account Credentials & Profile

  • Your name and email address upon voluntary registration.
  • Phone number (optional for SMS-based verification).
  • Profile avatar (optional, chosen or uploaded by you).
  • Chosen student handle or username.

b) Study & Learning Analytics

  • Completed practice quizzes, scores, and subject mastery breakdown.
  • Bookmarks, personal study notes, and saved revision cards.
  • Study planner schedules and active revision timer minutes.
  • XP points and rank positions displayed on the All-India Leaderboard.

c) Technical & Device Diagnostics

  • Device model and operating system version (for app compatibility).
  • App version and build number.
  • Anonymized crash reports and performance telemetry to resolve software bugs.
📌 Permissions: We do not access your contacts, camera, or personal documents outside the app without explicit permission.

2. 🎯 How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate educational purposes:

  • Account Authentication: Enable seamless cloud sync across all your phones and tablets.
  • Personalized Study Insights: Highlight your strengths and weak areas in NCERT and board exam modules.
  • Leaderboard Ranking: Compute your accuracy metrics and All-India percentile.
  • Support & Communication: Respond directly to support requests and send essential syllabus updates.
  • System Reliability: Diagnose technical crashes and ensure uninterrupted offline accessibility.
✅ We do not sell your data to brokers or advertisers.
✅ We do not share your contact information with predatory marketing firms.

3. 🛡️ Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using enterprise-grade security practices:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Managed on Supabase with encrypted Postgres databases.
  • End-to-End Transport Security: All data transmitted via SSL/TLS (HTTPS) encryption.
  • Credential Hashing: Passwords are salt-hashed and never stored in readable plain text.
  • Access Controls: Strict role-based backend authorization for engineering staff.

4. 🔗 Third-Party Integrations

Vidya Topper utilizes minimal, verified third-party services to deliver cloud services and optional login:

  • Supabase: For database storage, real-time sync, and user authentication. Supabase Privacy Policy →
  • Google Sign-In: Optional one-tap authentication through Google Services. Google Privacy Policy →
  • Google AdMob: Serves non-intrusive banner ads to maintain free access for all students.

5. 👦 Children's Privacy

Vidya Topper is an educational platform crafted for students from Class 6 upwards. We take proactive precautions to protect young learners:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental guidance.
  • The application contains zero adult content and adheres strictly to Google Play Families Policies.
  • Parents and guardians can request prompt deletion of their child's account by contacting us at vidyatopper.in@gmail.com.

6. 📶 Offline Storage & Downloads

Downloaded NCERT chapters, questions, and solution PDFs are stored inside your device's private app sandbox.

We do not inspect or upload locally cached files. Uninstalling the app completely purges all cached study materials from your device.

7. ✅ Your Rights & Control

You retain comprehensive rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Export: Request a digital copy of all records associated with your account.
  • Correction: Update your profile, name, class, and preferences at any time.
  • Account Deletion: Request permanent removal of your account, scores, and records from our database.

To exercise any data rights, write to vidyatopper.in@gmail.com.

8. 🔄 Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically to accommodate new educational features or regulatory adjustments. We will notify users of substantial revisions through an in-app prompt.
